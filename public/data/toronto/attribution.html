Original building data and downloads · Aerial imagery service · Processed coverage · Image georeferencing

Real multipatch surfaces are converted from the source coordinate system into the app's local metre frame. Roof photographs are geographically projected, not reconstructed photogrammetry. Facade materials and window lighting are illustrative. Photography and buildings have different dates; tall buildings may show roof lean or alignment artifacts. Imagery is downsampled for browser performance. The City supplies its context model for illustrative purposes and does not guarantee precision.
