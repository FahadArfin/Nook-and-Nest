Reconstruction scripts and editable Blender source are supplied in the Nook & Nest source repository: scripts/prepare-toronto-scenery.py and tools/blender/build_toronto_scenery.py. Derived mesh data can be reproduced from the source downloads; the geographic derivative remains ODbL.

What is estimated?

Footprints and available heights come from map data. When height is absent, floor count × 3.2 metres is used; otherwise 9 metres. Building parts retain their base heights; parent footprints with parts use a simplified 4-metre podium. Facade colors and window lighting are illustrative, not surveyed. Small footprint simplification is applied for browser performance. A 45-metre clear area leaves room for your editable home, which is placed about 80 metres above the waterfront.

Maps3D inspired the data approach; this model was built directly from open geographic data and does not copy Maps3D assets or retailer imagery.
