# Garage and shed collection research

This extension adds 55 distinct catalog entries across storage, workstations, tools, stationary machines, vehicle care, utilities, shed equipment and garage doors. Existing basic garage workbench, small tool cabinet, plain steel shelving, canister vacuum, step ladder, push mower, single-wheel barrow, basic sectional door, chest/upright freezers and utility safety details remain available. The new collection avoids simply recoloring them.

Research uses manufacturer and major retailer catalogs. Source drawings and published measurements inform construction; every geometry asset is original. No retailer media or marks are embedded. Model dimensions below are layout envelopes, including fixed handles, cords and brackets; their source basis is recorded individually in the machine-readable reference report. They are not installation or safety clearance claims.

The Craftsman drill-press page was rechecked on September 29: its published 10-inch size describes the tool class, and it lists 3.5-inch spindle travel. Our 380 × 600 × 880 mm complete model envelope is an authored choice. All 55 report links match the 45 source records and contain no unintended URL whitespace.

## Construction acceptance

- Cabinets need folded panel edges, consistent door/drawer gaps, distinct handles, visible feet and rear mounting construction. Wood tops use separate lamellae and real thickness.
- Racks must read as open structure: independent rails, braces, hangers, bolts and wire grids. Pegboard openings are modeled holes, not painted dots. Bins and utility sink have genuinely recessed interiors.
- Machines need recognizable castings, guards, switches, handles, motor ventilation, cable routing and battery packs. Wheels get hubs, sidewalls and authored tread at bounded cost. Saws are shown parked with guards; no animated tool operation.
- Garage door styles share the existing frame, aperture and full overhead hardware envelope. Glazing occupies real openings. Back-side hinges, rollers, torsion shaft and track returns remain modeled.
- Separate tabletop props remain independently placeable. Only real unobstructed shelf/worktop planes are offered as support surfaces. Rail-only tire/lumber racks deliberately do not claim a solid shelf plane.
- Wall and ceiling items remain independently placed, not automatically attached electrically or mechanically. The opener and keypad are static scene props. Riding mower, snowblower, generator, jack and welding cart are stored layout objects.

## Material direction

Deep slate powder-coated cabinets, muted red tool cases, warm laminated wood, satin steel, dark non-glossy rubber, and small measured safety-color accents. Different material keys separate finish surfaces from steel fixings, plastic housings, grips, glass and wood. Richness comes from material microstructure and construction detail rather than exaggerated noisy textures. Shared PBR texture integration is owned by the collection pipeline.

## Research limits

Catalog dimension terminology varies: product length may include or exclude handles, and garage-door width excludes trim and tracks. Published dimensions were converted where directly available; the remaining envelopes are explicitly original authored choices. The garage refrigerator and portable generator closely follow reference overall dimensions, while their recognizable construction details are newly authored. The snowblower intake and riding mower cutting deck are reference size classes, not claims of exact product replicas.

The selection draws from NewAge and Husky cabinetry, Gladiator overhead storage and appliances, Wall Control perforated panels, Rubbermaid FastTrack rails, BORA lumber arms, SafeRacks tire cradles, Akro-Mils bin carriers, Sterilite totes, Milwaukee modular cases, Werner ladders, DEWALT and Craftsman tools, Oneida dust separation, Lincoln Electric welding equipment, Reelcraft hose/cord management, Harbor Freight vehicle-care furniture, ChargePoint EV equipment, Mustee utility tubs, Rheem treatment cabinets, EGO yard machines, Gorilla carts, Wavian containers and Clopay/Chamberlain doorway systems. Dimensions and original construction choices are attached to each entry in the JSON report.

A practical single-car workshop can combine the narrow locker, folding bench, wall cabinet, pegboard, bins, charger and portable tools without consuming a full parking bay. A larger garage can use the hutch bench, drawer/corner base, ceiling rack, tire/lumber racks, stationary tools and service cart. Wet/utility equipment, yard machines and doorway hardware remain independently placeable. These are composition ideas; the app does not determine operational or installation clearances.
