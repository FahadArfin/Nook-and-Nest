# Landscape contracts

Current requirements grouped from the original guide. Later explicit directions take precedence over older batch notes. Read the latest-wins rules in AGENTS.md first.


Batch 8 visual feedback: vegetation must be very detailed, especially conifers. Use dense individual needles on branching shoots, species-specific leafy crowns, curved grass blades, fern leaflets and modeled flower clusters; simple stacked cones, sparse whorls and broad leaf cards are inadequate. Clocks need proper case silhouettes, legible dials/numerals and layered mechanisms/details. Fireplaces need distinct masonry/cast-iron/tiled construction, recessed fireboxes, firebrick, round bark logs, grates and convincing flames. Preserve all existing placement IDs and real dimensions when replacing models.

Batch 6 (September 2026): preserve the furniture the user already likes while adding distinct seating, beds, lighting, appliances/supplies, rugs/tables, pet furniture, bathroom fixtures/organizers, desk/pantry/nursery pieces, original anime art, candles/bowls/clocks, fireplaces and illuminated Christmas trees/presents. Batch 7 adds opt-in hill/hollow/river sculpting and more individually modeled vegetation. Keep foundations protected, terrain bounded, reduced-motion water animation, saved-data compatibility and one undo step per stroke. TV placement must include the original Picture TV and a precise support-surface chooser. Ordinary furniture edits must retain existing floor geometry and unchanged models instead of rebuilding the whole room.

Batch 5 covers Outdoor catalog plants, conifers/maples/cherry trees, patio furniture and barbecues (#9), opt-in bounded grass (#16), optional distant city/suburban/rural/farm/medieval dioramas (#23), and wall-free paving/path modules (#24). Keep plain ground and grass-off as the absent-setting defaults for all existing projects. Outdoor placement on the lowest layer must rest on ground or supported paving, not hover at apartment-floor height. Grass must not obstruct floor or furnishing footprints and must use a bounded instanced representation. Scenery is not selectable architecture; retain original Blender sources for the environment assets.

Floor tile editing must support drag-to-size rectangular previews for both painting and erasing. Keep changes uncommitted until the user confirms them with a model-anchored cancel/check toolbar, and record each confirmed region as one undo step. The environment outside the apartment should remain a clean plain ground plane without flowers, bushes, rocks, or other vegetation.

The user approved ChatGPT sign-in for private per-person online project libraries with recoverable versions. Preserve anonymous/offline local planning and JSON backups; distinguish local autosave from explicit online saves. Enforce ownership and stale-write protection on the server. Keep default scenery plain; the requested grass, background scenery, and outdoor vegetation are opt-in, never automatically added to existing plans.

Batch 9 quality preference: use the detailed Batch 8 botanical and furniture models as the accepted reference. Outdoor plants should have species-specific branching, folded veined leaves, shaped petals and legible flower centers; patio models need accurate joinery, woven or tailored construction and recognizable appliance details. Keep the handcrafted warmth while improving realism. Preserve IDs and real dimensions. Offer a grass/flower brush with adjustable spacing, a position preview, explicit confirmation and one undo per stroke; each planted piece remains independently editable. The remaining indoor model overhaul is a future pass, not part of this outdoor batch.

Model optimization preference: audit deployed models above 1 MB, prioritizing vegetation. Preserve aquarium fidelity exactly, including geometry, fish, interior plants, textures and animation. Prefer lossless mesh compression before simplifying any models; keep editable Blender originals, dimensions, IDs and placement compatibility. R2 remains deferred.

Bottom tools and sunlight (September 8): home editing lives in bottom drawers with one persistent dock; do not duplicate Paint tiles/Erase/Add wall navigation inside drawers. Keep land formation, vegetation and surroundings accessible there, and reserve the right inspector for selected furniture. Drawers ease up/down over 650 ms, switch by closing the old drawer then opening the new one, and respect reduced motion. Closing a dock tool returns to Arrange. Sun preview is opt-in, uses adjustable direction/elevation and illustrative presets, persists in projects with undo, and must not silently change wall visibility or camera framing.

Dense grass and floating tools: render painted grass in spatial GPU batches with preserved placement IDs, nearby detail, lighter distant blades, bounded grass-specific capacity and batched brush previews. Bottom menus should float as rounded rectangles above their invoking buttons with smooth reduced-motion-aware transitions. Land formation first opens Terrain, Plants and Landscape choices; only the chosen sub-menu expands. Water should use lightweight artistic ripples, depth colour and shoreline effects while preserving connected-basin filling and responsive terrain strokes.

Vegetation interaction: continuous high-density strokes should progress beyond the old 512-plant patch cap, retain one undo per stroke, and batch other outdoor plants while preserving individual edits. Use one Paint dock button with Walls/Floor choices. Landscape detail panels should be wide floating rounded rectangles like Paint, rather than narrow columns. Water should have restrained reflective ripples and depth texture while remaining inexpensive.

Water interaction preference: river sources must fill gradually; newly connected hollows attract existing water over time and raised terrain displaces it. Retain bounded simulation cost and protected foundations. Landscape details should use one wide paint-like workbench with its navigation inside; paint materials should start beside compact target/scope controls rather than below a mostly empty full-width row.

Water brush preference: new River strokes must add water without digging; flat ground should receive spreading water and slopes should guide downhill flow. Preserve older carved river saves. Reduce coarse shoreline artifacts while keeping simulation work bounded. Full-map grass should be evaluated as a compact coverage layer with camera-local detail, preserving existing individually editable plants.

Natural meadow preference: coverage grass must not repeat visible rows or identical per-tile patterns. Use stable world-space scatter and restrained size/orientation variation without changing saved density, nearby detail budgets or original individually placed models.
