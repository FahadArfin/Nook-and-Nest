# Bathroom collection — original design study

Research checked 2 September 2026. Retailer material informed the list of common
fixture types only. No retailer geometry, textures, logos, product names or
proprietary assets are used. Every piece has an original editable Blender source.

## Sources and distinctions

- [Kohler sink buying guide](https://experience.kohler.com/en/inspiration/buying-guides/bathroom-sinks-buying-guide): pedestal, vessel and wall-mounted sinks informed the standalone basin choices.
- [Kohler toilet buying guide](https://experience.kohler.com/en/inspiration/buying-guides/toilets-buying-guide): one-piece, two-piece, floor-mounted and wall-hung configurations informed three distinct toilet silhouettes.
- [Kohler bathtubs](https://www.kohler.com/en/products/bathtubs/shop-bathtubs): alcove, freestanding and clawfoot families informed the bath collection.
- [Kohler shower collection](https://www.us.kohler.com/us/browse/bathroom-showering-shower-stalls/_/N-2d8t): shower enclosures and bath screens informed the enclosed corner shower, open-entry shower and bath/shower combination.
- [IKEA bathroom mirrors](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/cat/bathroom-mirrors-20490/): plain wall mirrors and mirror cabinets informed the four mirror/storage choices.

## Implementation boundaries

19 original pieces: four mirrors/cabinets, six sinks/vanities, three toilets,
two standing showers and four bath arrangements. Sink and bath shells have real
recessed interiors. Glazing is translucent; mirror faces are stylized tinted
materials, not realtime reflections. Halo mirror trim is a light-style border,
not a switchable lamp. Doors, drawers, faucets and bath parts are static models.

Nominal dimensions describe the complete model envelope, including hardware,
not the rough-in or plumbing opening. Bathroom wall mounts start at sensible
editable heights, but alignment against the wall is manual. The vessel sink
can snap onto supported tables, base cabinets and prep counters; use manual
height for other surfaces. Vanity tops reuse the existing saved finish selector.

These are visual layout aids, not building-code, waterproofing, accessibility,
structural-support or plumbing-installation validation. Verify actual fixtures,
clearances and installation documents separately.
